The vision

London does not need louder politics. It needs steadier leadership.

This campaign is built around a simple belief: City Hall should be practical, responsive, and focused on the everyday things people actually feel — the cost of living, housing pressure, road conditions, public safety, local business, and trust in local government.

Strong roots. Clear priorities. Better outcomes for every neighbourhood.

Growing London well

Priorities built for real life.

01

Housing that moves faster

Speed up approvals, support smart density, and remove bottlenecks that make it harder to build homes Londoners can actually afford.

02

Affordability & accountability

Respect taxpayers by focusing spending on services that create visible value.

03

Safer communities

Coordinate practical safety, outreach, and enforcement so residents see action where it matters.

04

Fix the basics

Roads, transit, snow, parks, traffic, and service delivery should be reliable, not political theatre.

Meet Feher

A campaign built from the ground up.

Feher is running because London deserves practical leadership rooted in common sense, accountability, and pride in every neighbourhood.

The Forest City is more than a nickname. It is a reminder that strong cities need strong roots: families, small businesses, safe streets, reliable services, and leaders who listen before they talk.

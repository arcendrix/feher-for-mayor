The case for change

London is growing. The systems are not keeping up.

The next mayor has to be more than a symbol. The job is operational: align departments, set priorities, measure progress, communicate clearly, and make the city feel functional again.

The practical plan

Five pressure points. One disciplined city agenda.

01

Build homes faster

Speed approvals, support smart density, and focus on housing regular working families can actually afford.

02

Fix the daily commute

Coordinate construction better, improve major corridors, and treat traffic flow like a quality-of-life issue.

03

Restore downtown confidence

Make downtown cleaner, safer, more active, and easier for small businesses to believe in again.

04

Coordinate crisis response

Bring mental health, homelessness, addiction, outreach, enforcement, and housing supports into one accountable operating model.

05

Respect the taxpayer

Prioritize visible service improvements, publish clear metrics, and stop hiding weak execution behind vague language.

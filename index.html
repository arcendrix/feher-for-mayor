The message

London does not need another performance. It needs a better operator.

A serious mayoral campaign should feel clear, grounded, and trustworthy. This platform is built around the pressures Londoners actually feel every day: housing, traffic, downtown confidence, mental health, public safety, infrastructure, and affordability.
